# Public data used in this project

## Real (published by Government of Assam, NHM)

**NTEP (RNTCP) District RoP 2024-26, Programme Implementation Guideline**, National Health Mission Assam.
https://nhm.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/nhm_lipl_in_oid_6/menu/document/district_rop_2024-26_ntep.pdf

Files derived from it:

* `assam_district_ntep_rop_2024_26.csv` — per-district planning parameters for 2024-25, transcribed from the
  district tables in the RoP:
  * `tu_count` — Tuberculosis Units (activity 73.9, TU maintenance)
  * `tdc_count` — TB Detection Centres / DMCs (activity 73.10)
  * `npy_ds_target`, `npy_dr_target` — committed Nikshay Poshan Yojana beneficiaries, drug-sensitive and
    drug-resistant (activities 74.1, 74.2). Used as a proxy for expected patient load.
  * `acf_house_visit_target` — community-volunteer house-visit target for active case finding (activity 73.3)
  * `lab_tests_target` — lab consumables target for DMCs (activity 73.18); a proxy for diagnostic capacity
  * `ppsa_notification_target` — private-sector (PPSA) notification target (activity 75.4); 0 = district not in PPSA
  * `tribal_action_plan` — the six districts named in the RoP's DBT guideline as Tribal Action Plan districts
    (Baksa, Chirang, Dima Hasao, Kokrajhar, Karbi Anglong, Udalguri)
  * `hill_district` — Dima Hasao, Karbi Anglong, West Karbi Anglong (geographic classification)
* `assam_state_series_2016_2023.csv` — state-level performance table and notification and PMDT tables in the RoP.

**These are planning targets and committed allocations, not observed outcomes.** They say where the programme
expects workload and has put capacity. They do not say how many people died or were lost to follow-up in a district.

## Synthetic (generated by this repository, `tbds/synth`)

All patient-level records, facility-level (TU-level) performance, referral events, duplicates and death records are
**synthetic**. They are generated with plausible structure so the pipeline, models and dashboards can be built and
tested end to end. Nothing generated here may be read as a finding about real patients, facilities or districts.
Replace with a de-identified Nikshay extract (see `docs/DATA_ACCESS.md`) before any real use.
